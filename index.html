Blood tells you about the whole body. Bronchoalveolar lavage tells you about the airway itself, but a patient can only tolerate it a few times a year. The cell in between — airway-local and repeatable — is currently empty.

呼氣冷凝液（Exhaled Breath Condensate, EBC）是填這一格的候選方法：受試者對著冷凝管靜息呼吸約十分鐘，呼出氣體中的水蒸氣與來自氣道內襯液的微小液滴一起凝結成幾百微升的液體。非侵入、不需鎮靜、可以在門診椅上完成。

Exhaled breath condensate (EBC) is a candidate for that cell. The subject breathes quietly into a cooled tube for about ten minutes; water vapour and micro-droplets carried up from the airway lining fluid condense together into a few hundred microlitres of liquid. Non-invasive, no sedation, done in an outpatient chair.

01 ・ 基本定義01 ・ Definition

什麼是呼氣冷凝液？What is exhaled breath condensate?

歐洲呼吸學會（ERS）2017 年技術標準的定義是：「EBC 是讓呼出氣體接觸冷表面或冷凝器降溫後所取得的樣本，以液態或凍結狀態收集，用於分析其中的揮發性與非揮發性大分子。」

The European Respiratory Society 2017 technical standard defines it as: “EBC is obtained by cooling exhaled breath through contact with a cold surface or condenser. Samples are collected as fluid or frozen material and analysed immediately or later for volatile and non-volatile macromolecules.”

關鍵在於「它從哪裡來」。呼出氣流通過氣道時，剪切力會把覆蓋在氣道上皮表面的內襯液（airway lining fluid）撕出微小液滴，這些液滴隨氣流帶出體外。冷凝液裡絕大部分體積是水蒸氣，但真正有臨床意義的訊號，來自這些微滴所攜帶的蛋白、細胞因子與核酸。

The key question is where it comes from. As exhaled air travels through the airways, shear forces tear micro-droplets from the airway lining fluid coating the epithelium, and those droplets are carried out with the airstream. Most of the condensate volume is water vapour — but the clinically meaningful signal comes from what those droplets carry: proteins, cytokines and nucleic acids.

它不是唾液It is not saliva

唾液是 EBC 最主要的污染源。採樣時以限制唾液進入的流路設計加上「定期吞嚥」控制——這是 ERS 技術標準明文的操作要求，不是選配。

Saliva is the main contaminant. It is controlled by saliva-limiting flow-path design plus periodic swallowing during collection — an explicit requirement in the ERS standard, not an optional extra.

它不是痰It is not sputum

不需要高滲鹽水誘導、不需要咳出。病人只要靜息呼吸，因此重度阻塞或無法配合誘導痰的病人也能採。

No hypertonic saline induction, no expectoration. The patient only breathes quietly — so it is feasible in patients who cannot tolerate sputum induction.

它不是「一口氣」It is not “one breath”

這是最常見的誤解。EBC 是連續數分鐘的靜息潮氣呼吸；單次呼氣的水量遠不足以分析。把它說成「吹一口氣」會直接誤導臨床端對流程的預期。

The most common misunderstanding. EBC requires several minutes of continuous tidal breathing; a single exhalation yields far too little fluid. Calling it a “single breath test” misleads clinicians about the workflow.

它目前不是診斷工具It is not a diagnostic

全球尚無經核准的 EBC 體外診斷項目，也沒有公認的判讀斷點。本平台定位為研究用途（RUO），這一點我們在第一頁就說清楚。

There is no approved EBC in-vitro diagnostic anywhere, and no accepted cut-off values. This platform is research use only — stated up front rather than buried.

02 ・ 被忽略的一半02 ・ The overlooked half

如果您用過 EBC，您測的可能只是它的一半If you have used EBC before, you may have measured only half of it

過去二十多年的 EBC 臨床研究幾乎都走同一條路：以免疫分析逐一定量單一標記——pH、H₂O₂、8-isoprostane、白三烯、細胞激素。這條路有它的瓶頸，我們在第 05 節會把話講完：濃度常接近檢測下限、稀釋無法校正。

但同一份冷凝液裡還有另一層資訊，多數臨床端沒有用到：可同時測得的代謝物與揮發性化合物圖譜。這一層不是理論推測，是已經被量過、也已經被標準化方法量過的。

For more than two decades, EBC research has followed one route: quantifying single markers one at a time by immunoassay — pH, H₂O₂, 8-isoprostane, leukotrienes, cytokines. That route has limits, and we set them out in full in section 05: concentrations sit near the assay floor, and dilution cannot be corrected.

But the same condensate carries a second layer that most clinical work never touches: a simultaneously measurable profile of metabolites and volatile compounds. This is not speculation — it has been measured, and measured with a standardised method.

「你們連測到什麼分子都說不清楚」“You cannot even tell me which molecules”

這個問題可以分開回答。圖譜式分類可以在不做化合物定性的前提下進行（電子鼻與 breathprint 即屬此類）；需要機轉解釋時，再回到 GC-MS 做定性。前者用於分群與追蹤，後者用於解釋。取決於您的研究問題要回答什麼，兩者不衝突。

This splits into two answers. Profile-based classification can run without identifying compounds — that is what e-noses and breathprints do. When mechanism matters, GC-MS identification is added on top. The first is for grouping and tracking, the second for explanation. Which you need depends on your question; they do not conflict.

溫度不是工程細節，是變因Temperature is not a detail — it is a variable

一篇比較 5 °C 到 −56 °C 六個採集溫度的研究顯示，採集溫度會改變 EBC 中測得的揮發性與極性非揮發性化合物數量；作者同時指出，低溫下的增加也可能來自環境空氣冷凝造成的稀釋，而非真正的回收率提升。沒有一個「最佳溫度」可以照抄——但溫度必須穩定且可記錄，否則跨批次的圖譜無法比較。

A study comparing six collection temperatures from 5 °C to −56 °C found that collection temperature changes the number of volatile and polar non-volatile compounds detected; the authors also note the increase at colder settings may come from dilution by condensing ambient air rather than genuine recovery. There is no “optimal temperature” to copy — but temperature must be stable and logged, or profiles cannot be compared across runs.

台灣本地的進展What is happening in Taiwan

同樣依胸腔科相關性排序，每一格都對應已發表或已註冊的研究，並標出台灣本地已經走到哪裡。Ordered the same way. Each entry corresponds to published or registered work, with Taiwanese progress marked.

氣喘Asthma

非標定代謝體分析已在成人氣喘的 EBC 中找出疾病特異性圖譜，並以機器學習建立預測模型。傳統路線（EBC pH、白三烯、15-HETE、8-isoprostane）在兒童氣喘也已累積大量資料。

Untargeted metabolomics on EBC has identified disease-specific signatures in adult asthma, with machine-learning prediction built on them. The classical route — EBC pH, leukotrienes, 15-HETE, 8-isoprostane — is well documented in paediatric asthma.

台灣：已有醫學中心註冊 EBC 代謝體學臨床研究，兒科團隊亦發表過氣喘與非氣喘兒童的 EBC 代謝體差異。

Taiwan: EBC metabolomics studies are registered at local medical centres, and paediatric groups have published EBC metabolomic differences between asthmatic and non-asthmatic children.

COPD 與支氣管擴張COPD and bronchiectasis

2025 年一篇研究同時分析呼氣 VOC（volatolomics）與 EBC 代謝體，提出新的 COPD 診斷標記組合——這正是「兩層一起讀」的實例。EBC 蛋白體與 IL-8 等發炎指標的資料也已存在。

A 2025 study analysed breath VOCs and EBC metabolites together and proposed new diagnostic biomarker panels for COPD — a working example of reading both layers at once. EBC proteomic and IL-8 inflammation data also exist.

台灣：已有 COPD 與支氣管擴張的 EBC 代謝體註冊研究；另有 COPD 急性惡化 EBC IL-8 的本地發表。

Taiwan: EBC metabolomics in COPD and bronchiectasis is already registered locally, alongside published work on EBC IL-8 in COPD exacerbation.

阻塞性睡眠呼吸中止（OSA）Obstructive sleep apnoea

系統性回顧已整理呼氣分析在 OSA 的角色（FeNO、EBC、VOC 三條線）。臨床上的吸引力在於：多項睡眠檢查昂貴且耗時，而 CPAP 治療前後本來就需要客觀的追蹤指標。

A systematic review has mapped the role of breath analysis in OSA across FeNO, EBC and VOCs. The clinical appeal is concrete: polysomnography is expensive and slow, and CPAP therapy already needs objective follow-up measures.

台灣：這條線在台灣異常活躍——已有多年期計畫與連續發表，追蹤 CPAP 前後 EBC 中 resolvin、MMP-9、PGC1-α、SIRT1 的變化。

Taiwan: unusually active here — multi-year projects and a continuous publication record tracking resolvin, MMP-9, PGC1-α and SIRT1 in EBC before and after CPAP.

肺癌早期偵測Early lung cancer detection

EBC 中已測得 miRNA、來自肺組織的胞外囊泡、DNA 甲基化與 H₂O₂。英國 ExPeL 研究把 EBC 的 H₂O₂ 加上生理參數與機器學習，直接用在真實篩檢族群上；另有研究以 EBC 標記對照 LDCT 篩檢。

miRNA, lung-derived extracellular vesicles, DNA methylation and H₂O₂ have all been measured in EBC. The UK ExPeL study combined EBC H₂O₂ with physiological parameters and machine learning in a real screening population; other work benchmarks EBC markers against LDCT screening.

台灣：已有 EBC 與氣管沖洗液 DNA 甲基化用於肺癌早篩的計畫；電子鼻肺癌分類也已完成多中心交叉驗證。

Taiwan: projects using DNA methylation in EBC and airway washings for early lung cancer detection, plus multicentre cross-site validation of e-nose lung cancer classification.

重症、ARDS 與呼吸器相關肺炎Critical care, ARDS and VAP

機械通氣病人可直接在呼吸器吐氣端採集，全程不接觸病人——這是 EBC 在重症唯一無可取代的優勢。已有研究以 EBC 搭配拉曼光譜嘗試早期區辨呼吸器相關肺炎。

In ventilated patients the collector attaches to the expiratory limb, with no patient contact at all — the one advantage EBC has in critical care that nothing else matches. Studies have paired EBC with Raman spectroscopy to distinguish ventilator-associated pneumonia early.

台灣：已有急性肺損傷與敗血症預後的 EBC 註冊研究，以及胸腔外科術後肺損傷的多年期計畫。

Taiwan: registered EBC studies on acute lung injury and sepsis outcome prediction, plus multi-year projects on post-thoracic-surgery lung injury.

職業與環境暴露Occupational and environmental exposure

EBC 可重複、無侵入的特性，讓它特別適合暴露前後的配對設計——這類研究不需要疾病判讀斷點，因此是最容易起步的題型之一。

Because EBC is repeatable and non-invasive, it suits paired before-and-after exposure designs particularly well. These studies need no disease cut-off, which makes them among the easiest to start.

台灣：本地累積相當可觀——奈米微粒作業人員、廟宇工作者、交通警察空污暴露、塵肺症呼氣檢測均有發表。

Taiwan: a substantial local record — nanoparticle-exposed workers, temple workers, traffic police air-pollution exposure and pneumoconiosis breath testing have all been published.

台灣不是這個領域的旁觀者Taiwan is not a bystander in this field

ClinicalTrials.gov 上可查到台灣醫學中心自 2011 年起註冊的 EBC 代謝體學臨床研究（氣喘、COPD 與支氣管擴張、急性肺損傷、敗血症、胸腔手術）；政府研究資訊系統（GRB）另可查到橫跨 OSA、兒童氣喘、肺癌早篩，乃至自製 EBC 採集器的多年期計畫。台大團隊建置並發表的 Human Breathomics Database 與 Clinical Breathomics Dataset 也已公開。

這件事對您的意義是：如果您要做這個題目，在台灣不是第一個，前面已經有可以引用的本地前例與可以請教的同儕。

ClinicalTrials.gov lists EBC metabolomics studies registered by Taiwanese medical centres since 2011 — asthma, COPD and bronchiectasis, acute lung injury, sepsis, thoracic surgery. The government research information system (GRB) additionally lists multi-year projects spanning OSA, paediatric asthma, early lung cancer detection and even in-house EBC collector development. The Human Breathomics Database and the Clinical Breathomics Dataset, both built and published by a Taiwanese team, are publicly available.

What this means for you: if you take on this question, you are not the first in Taiwan. There is local precedent to cite and there are peers to ask.

05 ・ 臨床證據05 ・ Clinical evidence

包含一個大型的陰性結果Including one large negative result

The first study below is a negative result — and the largest one on this question. We put it first rather than leave you to find it.

陰性結果Negative

把 BALF 的分析物直接搬到 EBC，失敗了Porting a BALF analyte straight into EBC failed

476

在 476 份 EBC 與 444 份配對支氣管肺泡灌洗液（肺移植受贈者 197 人、血液惡性腫瘤 133 人）中，EBC 的半乳甘露聚醣（GM）與配對 BAL 沒有顯著相關；ROC 分析在任何族群都找不到可用的診斷斷點。

Across 476 EBC and 444 matched BAL specimens (197 lung transplant recipients, 133 haematologic malignancy patients), EBC galactomannan showed no significant correlation with matched BAL, and ROC analysis found no usable cut-off for invasive aspergillosis in any population.

Bhimji A, Bhaskaran A, Singer LG, et al. Clin Microbiol Infect. 2018;24(6):640–645. doi:10.1016/j.cmi.2017.09.018
陽性訊號Positive signal

換成核酸偵測之後，訊號出現了Switch the analyte, and the signal appears

10 天days

80 位接受高強度化療的急性白血病患者，於嗜中性球低下期每週兩次採集 EBC，以數位液滴 PCR 偵測麴菌 DNA：敏感度 50%、特異度 90.5%，與血清 GM（50%／96.7%）相當；其中一例在臨床診斷前 10 天即測得陽性。

In 80 acute leukaemia patients on intensive chemotherapy, EBC was collected twice weekly during neutropenia and tested by droplet digital PCR for Aspergillus DNA: 50% sensitivity, 90.5% specificity — comparable to serum galactomannan (50% / 96.7%). In one patient EBC was positive 10 days before clinical diagnosis.

Bitterman R, Arora S, Ng CF, et al. Open Forum Infect Dis. 2024;11(10):ofae537. doi:10.1093/ofid/ofae537
重症應用Critical care

通氣病人可在呼吸器端採集，完全不接觸病人In ventilated patients, sampled at the circuit — no patient contact

在機械通氣的重症病人中，採樣裝置直接接在呼吸器吐氣端或吐氣孔，不需接觸病人。研究發現 EBC 中的 IL-2 與胸部 X 光評分相關、TNF-α 與肺炎診斷相關。作者明列限制：樣本數小、單中心、族群異質。

In mechanically ventilated ICU patients the collector attaches to the expiratory limb or exhalation port, requiring no patient contact. EBC IL-2 correlated with chest radiograph scores and TNF-α with a pneumonia diagnosis. The authors state the limits plainly: small sample, single centre, heterogeneous population.

Davis MD, Winters BR, Madden MC, et al. J Breath Res. 2020;15(1):016011. doi:10.1088/1752-7163/abc235

這三篇合起來說的是同一件事Read together, these three say one thing

EBC 的成敗不在「怎麼採」，在「採來測什麼」。把灌洗液既有的蛋白分析物原封不動搬到 EBC——因為濃度低、稀釋不定——會失敗；改用對低濃度容忍度高的方法（核酸擴增、質譜／離子遷移譜這類圖譜式分析），訊號就出得來。這也是我們把研發重心放在圖譜式分析與模式識別、而不是單一蛋白定量的原因。

Whether EBC works is decided less by how you collect it than by what you measure in it. Transplanting an established protein assay from lavage fluid fails, because concentrations are low and dilution is variable; methods tolerant of low concentration — nucleic acid amplification, or pattern-based analysis such as mass spectrometry and ion mobility spectrometry — recover the signal. That is why our development focuses on spectral pattern analysis and pattern recognition rather than single-protein quantification.

EBC 目前公認的三個弱點（引自 ERS 2017 技術標準）Three acknowledged weaknesses of EBC (from the ERS 2017 technical standard)

  1. 稀釋無法校正。Dilution cannot be corrected. 冷凝液佔呼出氣體體積的比例每次都不同，且尚未證實用稀釋因子標準化能改善再現性。 The proportion of exhaled volume that condenses differs every time, and normalising by a dilution factor has not been shown to improve reproducibility.
    「1 mL 的 EBC 完全不能視為一個標準化的生物檢體，因為每次採集過程中冷凝液佔呼出氣體體積的百分比並不固定。」“1 mL EBC cannot be considered a standardised biological specimen at all, because the percentage of condensed liquid of the exhaled volume is not constant for each collection process.”
  2. 多數標記接近檢測下限。Most markers sit near the assay floor. 這直接放大了分析變異，也是上面那篇陰性研究失敗的主因之一。 This amplifies analytical variability, and is one reason the negative study above failed.
  3. 跨實驗室缺乏標準化。No cross-laboratory standardisation. 採樣方法與標記物量測都尚未在不同實驗室間完成驗證。ERS 該份文件並明言：「本報告無意提供疾病診斷與處置的臨床指引。」 Neither collection methods nor marker assays have been validated across laboratories. The ERS document states explicitly: “This report is not intended to provide clinical guidance on disease diagnosis and management.”

設計上的一個建議One suggestion on design

如果沒有特別理由，我們建議第一個研究從單次採樣、不需回診的設計開始。縱貫追蹤會同時放大收案、排程與流失率三種負擔，而 EBC 的圖譜品質要到第一批樣本上了 GC-IMS 或 GC-MS 才看得出來。先確認訊號，再談追蹤設計，通常比較順。 Unless there is a specific reason not to, we suggest the first study uses a single collection visit with no follow-up. Longitudinal designs multiply recruitment, scheduling and attrition burden at once — and the quality of the EBC spectra is not visible until the first batch has run on GC-IMS or GC-MS. Confirming the signal first, then designing follow-up, usually goes more smoothly.

分析範圍的界線：我們只做揮發性代謝體The boundary of what we analyse: volatile metabolites only

我們能提供的分析只有揮發性代謝體（VOC），以 GC-IMS 或 GC-MS 進行。EBC 檢體中的其他生化項目——細胞因子、pH、H₂O₂、8-isoprostane、核酸、蛋白定量等——須由貴院既有的實驗室設備或委外管道執行，我們不提供這些檢測，也不代為委外。若您的研究設計需要這些指標作為對照或共同終點，建議在計畫書撰寫階段就先確認院內量能與費用來源。

此外，本平台為研究用途（RUO）：我們回饋的是化學圖譜與分析結果，不產生任何個別受試者的疾病判讀，也不應寫入病歷或回報給受試者作為臨床資訊。

The only analysis we can provide is of volatile metabolites (VOCs), by GC-IMS or GC-MS. Every other assay on an EBC specimen — cytokines, pH, H₂O₂, 8-isoprostane, nucleic acids, protein quantification — must run on your institution’s own laboratory equipment or through your own outsourcing route. We do not perform these assays and cannot arrange them on your behalf. If your design needs any of them as a comparator or co-primary endpoint, confirm in-house capacity and funding while the protocol is being written.

This platform is also research use only: what we return are chemical spectra and analytical results. It produces no disease interpretation for any individual subject, and should not enter the medical record or be reported back to subjects as clinical information.
